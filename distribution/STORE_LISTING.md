## Description

Make space for work, study, practice, and downtime with Weekdeck.

Build your week with reusable blocks, adjust each day's planning hours, and move or resize activities as plans change. Copy a finished day to another day, choose a theme, and switch between a weekly desktop view and a focused phone layout.

Sign in to keep your planner in sync across devices. On iPhone and Android, enable weekly planning reminders and block transition notifications. Reminder changes made elsewhere refresh when the mobile app is opened again.
